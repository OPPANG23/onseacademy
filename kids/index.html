02 — THE WHY

More than choreography.

We use dance to help kids discover what they can do.

Every step is an opportunity to practice confidence, discipline, creativity, teamwork and respect. The goal is not simply to learn a routine — it is to give children a place to be seen, supported and proud of their progress.

14 — FAQ

Questions parents
usually ask.

How old can my child join?+

Our sample program structure begins at age 3. Confirm the current age groups and placement options during enrollment.

Do beginners need dance experience?+

No experience is needed for beginner pathways. Placement should be based on age, confidence and current skill level.

What should they wear?+

Comfortable clothing that allows free movement and appropriate footwear for the class. Final class-specific guidance can be added here.

Can parents watch?+

Parent viewing policies can vary by class and venue. Please confirm the current policy before your first session.
