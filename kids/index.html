01 — ABOUT US

A place to move
and become.

A child-first dance environment built around joyful learning, meaningful growth and the belief that every child has something special to share.

At Onse Academy, we believe every child deserves a space where they can express themselves, stay active, build friendships and discover the joy of movement.

Through dance, children develop confidence, discipline, creativity, teamwork and the courage to keep growing — from their first steps to performance-ready choreography.

Our mission is simple: to help children discover their gifts, grow through dance and use their talents for the glory of God.

05 — FAQ

Questions parents
usually ask.

How old can my child join? +

Our children's programs begin with age groups designed for different stages of development. Please confirm current availability and placement options through enrollment.

Do beginners need dance experience? +

No experience is needed for beginner pathways. Children are welcomed into a supportive environment where they can learn at their own pace.

What should they wear? +

Comfortable clothing that allows free movement and appropriate footwear for the class. Final class-specific guidance can be confirmed before the first session.

Can parents watch? +

Parent viewing policies may vary depending on the class and venue. Please contact Onse Academy for the current policy.

How much is tuition? +

For the latest tuition information and available classes, please contact Onse Academy or complete the enrollment form.

Where are classes held? +

Class locations and schedules may vary. Please contact Onse Academy or complete the enrollment form for the latest details.

Where can I enroll? +

You can enroll your child through the official Onse Academy enrollment form. Click the button below to get started.
